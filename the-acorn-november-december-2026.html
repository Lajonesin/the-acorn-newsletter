Meeting Recap

Monday, November 16

Here's a look at what we covered at the November general meeting, including the questions neighbors asked. If a question didn't get an answer that night, we've marked it "to follow" and will circle back.

Board updates

  • Minutes and finances
    The September minutes were approved. The Treasurer reported October income of $326,677, expenses of $211,243, and cash and investments of $2,385,287. The full report is in the Financial Snapshot above.
  • Potholes
    Bids have been received and the board approved moving forward with the repairs.
  • Front entrance cleanup
    A small cleanup is planned for the entrance: light trimming, moving rocks, setting pavers under the bench, new flowers, and removing dead plants. If you live near the front, you may be asked to remove pots or planters.
  • Roof repairs
    The start was delayed. Work begins with the small laundry room, which needs shingles only. Residents will be notified before work moves to the buildings.
  • Transformers
    Three transformers on the property are leaking oil at the base. They will be replaced one at a time, so all three will not be done at once. Residents will be given advance notice.
  • Parking lot leak
    The repair is being scheduled. The office will announce the start date once it is set.
  • Community map
    A large new map will be installed on the side of the building once it arrives. The QR code map at the guard booth has been installed. Scan it, enter a unit number, and it shows how to get there.
  • Bridge
    The bridge is unsafe. The neighboring community association has no plans to make it usable and will likely remove it.
  • Violations
    Inspections continue, and fences are next. Please keep stairways and the area at your door clear so that emergency crews can get through. If you receive a letter and need more time, please contact PMI at (281) 556-5111. Letters begin as courtesy notices, followed by a second letter and then a formal notice with time to correct.
  • Annual meeting and election
    Voting has begun. Ballot packets were mailed to owners, and emails with a link for electronic voting went out Friday, November 20. Online voting ends December 6 at 4 p.m. The annual meeting is December 7.

Security discussion

  • Concerns raised
    Residents raised concerns about trespassers walking onto the property and about guard attentiveness and patrols. The board said it is reviewing the security setup and will seek bids for a new security service.
  • Our guards
    Our guards are unarmed, and armed guards are not possible because our insurance does not allow them. They help with parking passes, announce visitors, keep a log of visitors, send after-hours emergency requests to maintenance, and serve as a general contact for residents when needed. Current staffing is one guard in the morning, one in the afternoon, and two overnight. The gates cannot be locked permanently because people need a way out in an emergency.
  • How you can help
    Call 911 for anything urgent, and do not rely on the guards for emergencies. For repeated concerns, send the office the date, time, and details so the pattern can be passed to HPD. You can also report non-emergency concerns to Houston 311, which helps document activity in our area. You can also ask HPD for extra patrols in our area through its Citizen Alert page. The more requests HPD receives from our community, the more likely officers are to patrol here. Please do not confront anyone. HPD also holds a monthly community meeting at Regency Square that anyone may attend.

Resident Questions

Answered and to follow

Are there rules for holiday decorations?
Answered at the end of the meeting. There is no official rule, so please see the Holiday Season section for the safety reminders.

Will dues go up 10%?
Answered. Board members said the 10% figure heard on Facebook is not correct. An increase tied to inflation is planned for next year, about 3.5%. The increase will go toward roof replacements, the paving project, transformer replacements, security updates, and general upkeep and maintenance of the property. The full budget will be shared in December.

Will reserve funding change?
Answered. The reserve contribution that lenders look for is rising in January, and the board will try to meet it.

Could electronic parking passes or remote monitoring replace the guards?
Answered. Our guards are unarmed. They help with parking passes, announce and log visitors, send after-hours emergency requests to maintenance, and serve as a general contact for residents. The gate openings are small, and a gate system needs electrical boxes and underground work. Another community with 176 units took over a year to set up its system. The board is looking at technology such as scanning IDs for visitor passes, and has made no decision.

Can residents personalize patios and staircases?
Answered. Some flexibility is expected as long as access stays clear. Changes to the rules follow a legal process.

What if someone is sleeping on a landing or looking in windows?
Answered. Call 911 if you are in danger. Otherwise, report the date, time, and place to the office so the pattern can be documented for HPD.

I cannot submit a request in the Vantaca portal without a photo.
Partly answered. A QR code for the portal is in this issue, and the office can help with sign-up. The photo requirement is to follow.

Can the cat leash requirement be changed?
Answered. No. The leash requirement is a standing rule under our bylaws and the City of Houston. Any pet outdoors must be on a leash, and pet cats are not permitted to roam freely. This does not apply to feral cats that are not living as pets.

Holiday Season

Reminders for the season

The holiday season is here. As we celebrate the season of gratitude, let's remember the people who help make our community a wonderful place to live.

If you see a member of our maintenance crew or office staff this season, take a moment to say thank you. Their hard work helps keep The Oaks running smoothly, and a little appreciation can mean a lot.

  • Packages and guests
    Holiday guests and deliveries are on their way! Please keep guest spaces open for visitors, and share your unit number or the unit finder link below so drivers and family can find you easily.
  • Holiday decorations
    We love seeing neighbors get into the holiday spirit! The Association does not have an official decorating rule, so these are friendly courtesy requests and no one will be fined. We're counting on good judgment and courtesy. Please keep decorations neat and tidy, and take them down within two weeks after the holidays. Nothing may be placed in a common area without the Association's consent.
  • Decorating safely
    Let's keep the season bright and safe. Please use UL-listed lights and outdoor-rated extension cords, and avoid overloading outlets. Turn lights off when you leave or head to bed. Please keep decorations out of walkways, stairs, and doorways, and make sure nothing blocks or gets in the way of anyone's movement, including neighbors with strollers, walkers, or wheelchairs, and emergency responders. Candles and open flames are not safe on balconies or patios. Please don't nail, screw, or staple anything to the buildings or common-area trees, and don't plug into common-area outlets. If you have a live tree, keep it watered and remove it promptly.

Project Committees

How projects get started

The Landscaping Committee is a project committee, not a standing landscaping committee. It was formed for the current landscaping project and will disband once the project is completed. The committee works with the Board and management and makes recommendations to the Board.

Owners are welcome to suggest projects or request that an item be considered. The Board will determine whether a request falls within the project's scope, budget, and timeline.

Landscaping updates

Tree removal and stump grinding at Building 12 have been completed.

Beautifying our entrance

We are making efforts to beautify and enhance our entrance area, with more to come. Thank you for your patience as we work through projects in a careful, orderly way.

A new landscape request form is in development Coming soon

Before making any landscaping change, and before asking for a removal or a planting, residents will be asked to use a new landscape request form. We will announce when it is available. Each request will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

Keeping common areas clean and groomed

You can still ask the landscapers for help keeping common areas clean and groomed. Please submit those requests through Vantaca.

Please remember: common areas may not be changed or added to without written consent from the Board. Anything planted without approval may be removed.

Items that need to stay out of common areas

Metal objects, statues, bird baths, feeders, and similar items must be kept out of common areas. They are hazards to our landscapers and prevent mowing, weeding, and edging. Residents may be asked to remove them. If you receive a notice, please follow the instructions to avoid fines.
