Thanksgiving Week

Thank you, and a few reminders

Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26. Please join us in thanking our maintenance crew and office staff for their work keeping The Oaks running. A kind word goes a long way.

  • Packages and guests
    Expect busier gates and more deliveries. Please leave guest parking open for visitors, and give drivers your unit number or the unit finder link below.
  • Holiday decorations
    The Association does not have an official rule on holiday decorations, so we are asking for common sense and courtesy. Please keep decorations tidy, and take them down within two weeks after the holidays. Nothing may be placed in the common areas without the Association's consent.
  • Decorating safely
    Use UL-listed lights and extension cords rated for outdoor use. Do not overload outlets, and turn lights off when you leave or go to bed. Keep walkways, stairs, doorways, and exits clear of cords and decorations. No open flames on balconies or patios, including candles. Please do not nail, screw, or staple anything to the buildings or common-area trees, and do not plug anything into common-area outlets. If you have a live tree, keep it watered and remove it promptly.

Landscaping Committee

Caring for our common areas

The Landscaping Committee works with the Board and management to care for, improve, and keep consistent the landscaping in our common areas. The committee reviews landscaping concerns and requests and makes recommendations to the Board.

Update: tree removal and stump grinding

Tree removal and stump grinding at Building 12 have been completed.

Beautifying our entrance

We are making efforts to beautify and enhance our entrance area, with more to come. Thank you for your patience as we work through projects in a careful, orderly way.

A new landscape request form is in development Coming soon

Before making any landscaping change, and before asking for a removal or a planting, residents will be asked to use a new landscape request form. We will announce when it is available. Each request will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

Keeping common areas clean and groomed

You can still ask the landscapers for help keeping common areas clean and groomed. Please submit those requests through Vantaca.

Please remember: common areas may not be changed or added to without written consent from the Board. Anything planted without approval may be removed.

Items that need to stay out of common areas

Metal objects, statues, bird baths, feeders, and similar items must be kept out of common areas. They are hazards to our landscapers and prevent mowing, weeding, and edging. Residents may be asked to remove them. If you receive a notice, please follow the instructions to avoid fines.

Things to Do Around Town

Holiday events for neighbors

Looking for something to do close to home? Here are a few holiday events in the Westchase area.

Paws Up for Santa at Sneed Dog Park
Saturday, November 21, 10 a.m. to noon, 9855 Pagewood Lane. Photos with Santa for your pup, coffee and donuts, giveaways, and a caricature artist. The Harris County Pets Mobile Adoption Bus will be on site with dogs available for adoption for $20 cash.

Grinch Outdoor Movie Nights
Friday, December 4 at Woodchase Park ("The Grinch") and Saturday, December 5 at Camden Park ("How the Grinch Stole Christmas"). Both run 5 to 8 p.m., with the movie starting at 6. Lights, lawn games, coloring, and popcorn, with food trucks at Camden Park. Parking is next door at the Robinson-Westchase Neighborhood Library.

Camden Park Christmas Celebration
Saturday, December 12, morning. Photos with Santa, live performances, a DJ, balloon artist, face painting, ornament making and crafts, an inflatable slide, and food trucks.
