Honoring Our Veterans

On Wednesday, November 11, we observe Veterans Day. To the veterans who live at The Oaks, and to the family members who served alongside them, thank you for your service and for the sacrifices you and your families have made.

We honor those who served in every branch of the United States Armed Forces: the Army, Marine Corps, Navy, Air Force, Space Force, and Coast Guard.

Take a moment around Veterans Day to say hello to a veteran neighbor and thank them in person. Small gestures matter, and they help make The Oaks a place where neighbors look out for one another.

Landscaping Committee

Caring for our common areas

The Landscaping Committee works with the Board and management to care for, improve, and keep consistent the landscaping in our common areas. The committee reviews landscaping concerns and requests and makes recommendations to the Board.

Beautifying our entrance

We are making efforts to beautify and enhance our entrance area, with more to come. Thank you for your patience as we work through projects in a careful, orderly way.

A new landscape request form is in development Coming soon

Before making any landscaping change, and before asking for a removal or a planting, residents will be asked to use a new landscape request form. We will announce when it is available. Each request will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

Keeping common areas clean and groomed

You can still ask the landscapers for help keeping common areas clean and groomed. Please submit those requests through Vantaca.

Please remember: common areas may not be changed or added to without written consent from the Board. Anything planted without approval may be removed.

Items that need to stay out of common areas

Metal objects, statues, bird baths, feeders, and similar items must be kept out of common areas. They are hazards to our landscapers and prevent mowing, weeding, and edging. Residents may be asked to remove them. If you receive a notice, please follow the instructions to avoid fines.
